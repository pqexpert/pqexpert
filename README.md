## Research directions

- **Post-quantum readiness:** cryptographic inventories, migration planning, and the operational work required to adopt new standards.
- **Continuous security validation:** DevSecOps, automated assessment, and evidence that helps teams understand and reduce risk.
- **Resilient institutions and infrastructure:** how governance, procurement, and technical design shape dependable systems.

These are ongoing areas of research and development. Individual repositories describe their implemented scope and verification status.

## How I work

Evidence before hype. Privacy by design. Clear provenance, useful documentation, and claims proportional to what has been demonstrated.

I welcome substantive discussion, reproducible findings, and thoughtful contributions through the public projects above.
